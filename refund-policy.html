Refund policy

At Rama Skill Academy, we strive to provide the best learning experience. However, we understand that circumstances may change. This Refund Policy outlines the terms and conditions for course fee refunds.Rama Skill Academy is an educational initiative of Rama Foundation.

Refund eligibility

Refund requests must be submitted within 7 days of course enrollment. After this period, no refunds will be processed. Refunds are only applicable if you have not accessed more than 20% of the course content.

Refund process

To request a refund, please contact our support team with your enrollment details and reason for the refund request. Approved refunds will be processed within 14 business days to the original payment method.

Non-refundable items

Registration fees, course materials that have been downloaded, and certification fees are non-refundable. Special promotional discounts and offers are also non-refundable.

Contact us

If you have any questions about our refund policy, please contact us through our contact page or email us directly.
